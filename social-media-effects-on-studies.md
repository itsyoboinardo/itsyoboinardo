## Summary of Key Findings

### Negative Effects:
- Reduced attention span and concentration
- Lower GPA in heavy social media users
- Increased procrastination
- Sleep disruption affecting academic performance
- Multitasking reduces learning efficiency
- FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) causing anxiety

### Positive Effects:
- Enhanced collaboration and peer learning
- Access to educational resources and communities
- Improved digital literacy skills
- Networking opportunities with experts
- Quick information sharing and problem-solving
- Support groups for academic challenges

### Recommendations:
- Set specific times for social media use
- Use website blockers during study sessions
- Practice mindful technology consumption
- Leverage social media for educational purposes
- Balance online and offline study methods
